About EasyBeauty

A digital marketplace built to help women find trusted beauty professionals — with confidence.

The Problem

Many women currently rely on Instagram, WhatsApp, referrals and word-of-mouth to find hairstylists, makeup artists, nail technicians and lash technicians. This makes it hard to compare services and prices, check reviews, or book with confidence — everything lives in scattered DMs and stories.

Our Solution

EasyBeauty brings beauty professionals together in one simple platform. Customers can search by service and location, view detailed profiles with portfolios and real reviews, and send a booking request in minutes — then connect directly on WhatsApp to confirm.

Our Pilot Market

We're starting in Gombe State, Nigeria, working with a small community of hairstylists, makeup artists, nail technicians and lash technicians to prove the concept before expanding to more cities.

What We Value

🤝

Trust

Real profiles, real reviews — no more guessing who's behind the DM.

✨

Simplicity

Search, compare and book in a few taps, no clutter or confusion.

💖

Empowerment

Helping beauty professionals get discovered and grow their business.
